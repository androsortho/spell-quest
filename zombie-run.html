ZOMBIE RUN

Two lanes. Tap a side, swipe, or use the arrow keys to switch. Your squad fires on its own.

Green gates add or multiply your soldiers. Shoot a gate to grow its number before you reach it.
Red gates subtract or divide. Pick the lane with the better math.
Break the crate in your lane to grab the weapon inside.
Hordes stop the road. Switch lanes and hold the line.
